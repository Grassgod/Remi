MUL-395: Archive Count Race Fix

The two grouped query functions now reject obsolete cross-key archive-count writes. The fix covers canceled requests, uncanceled responses arriving out of order, and newer optimistic count updates.

Before: e13655427bce8d0cb0420757b3d4516abab5606b
Tested product source: b4954b18ffbd3a48be1648602459255d9d0b79ab
Merged main: 01810898c7df0374d3784b8791a8f1ed72002b09, without conflicts.

Publication Rules

A latest-request marker is shared per QueryClient/workspace, independent of list filter keys. Publication requires the latest marker, an un-aborted signal, and an unchanged count dataUpdateCount. No clock comparison is used. A failed newest request keeps the existing count and does not let an older response publish.

The signal is read at publication time to preserve TanStack's initial-request sharing during temporary observer removal. List requests, grouping, pagination and the API-client 404 fallback keep their existing semantics.
